        How it works

        Why a test, not a score

        A score guesses. A test watches AI customers get your details right, and fail on a broken twin.

        Read

        Muse Spark reads your pages and their images, and drafts your hours, services, prices and booking link, citing where each came from.

        Prove

        Three Meta models act as skeptical customers. Every answer is checked against your data and your live calendar, and a deliberately broken twin has to fail.

        Install

        One script tag exposes them to AI agents as WebMCP tools. Bookings always open a confirm step on your page; nothing books without a human click.
